# GrillRoom Architectural Design Note

## 1. Executive Summary
GrillRoom is an adversarial venture capital pitch simulator built on Next.js 15, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Neon Postgres, and the Groq API. Unlike generic conversational simulators, GrillRoom enforces deterministic accountability: every claim made by the founder is tracked in an active Due Diligence Ledger, investor turn decisions are orchestrated by a rule-based planner, and investment verdicts are computed algorithmically in pure code rather than generated by an LLM.

---

## 2. Core Engine Architecture

### A. The Two-Model Split
- **Diligence Analyst (Fast Model: 20B parameters)**:
  - Triggered synchronously on founder answer submission.
  - Scores answers on 5 dimensions (directness, specificity, evidence, logic, honesty).
  - Extracts new claims and identifies metric contradictions.
  - Generates recommended conviction deltas (clamped to ±15).
- **Investor Persona Generation (Nuanced Model: 120B parameters)**:
  - Streams in-character boardroom interrogation speech via Server-Sent Events (SSE).
  - Strictly grounded in investor priorities, rubrics, and the active claim ledger.
  - Founder text is treated as raw untrusted data inside `<founder_text>` delimiters.

### B. The Due Diligence Ledger
- Extracts every factual assertion into structured records (`id`, `session_id`, `category`, `claim_text`, `status`, `thread_state`, `ladder_level`, `source_turn`).
- Status state machine:
  - `unverified`: Initial state of newly stated claims.
  - `evidenced`: Founder provided verifiable numbers or sources (mapped to Verified or Strong Claim).
  - `contradicted`: Conflicting metrics or mutually exclusive assertions detected.
  - `conceded`: Founder acknowledged an assertion was ungrounded.
- Thread management tracks drilling depth (`ladder_level`) and consecutive followups used.

### C. The Deterministic Planner
Decides the next investor to speak without LLM intervention:
1. **Interrupt Rule**: Contradictions with severity $\ge 3$ trigger an immediate interrupt by the domain specialist.
2. **Dodge Rule**: Vague or evasive answers cause the active investor to escalate questioning ladder depth.
3. **Fairness Rule**: Investors with fewer spoken turns receive priority to ensure balanced room dynamics.
4. **Cross-Talk Rule**: Inter-investor reactions occur when partner conviction scores diverge by $\ge 30$ points.
5. **Turn Cap**: Maximum 2 consecutive turns per investor in tough mode (1 in friendly, 3 in shark).

### D. Conviction Model & Algorithmic Verdicts
- Each investor maintains a conviction score strictly clamped to $[0, 100]$.
- Deltas are sanity-checked against answer scores (poor answers cannot increase score; excellent answers cannot decrease score).
- Verdict thresholds are evaluated strictly in TypeScript:
  - **Tough (Default)**: In $\ge 65$, Conditional $40-64$, Out $< 40$.
  - **Friendly**: In $\ge 60$, Conditional $35-59$, Out $< 35$.
  - **Shark**: In $\ge 72$, Conditional $45-71$, Out $< 45$.
- Simulated term sheets are derived algorithmically from stated ask amounts and final conviction scores.

### E. Progression & Debrief Loop
- Session retry links child sessions to parent sessions via `parent_session_id`.
- Side-by-side comparison (`/compare/[id]`) computes conviction deltas, criteria improvements, and resolution of previously unverified threads.
