A sharp wedge targets an acutely underserved buyer with a hyper-focused solution that incumbents ignore. Winning the wedge builds proprietary workflow data and brand authority, opening adjacent enterprise tiers. When pitching, clearly articulate who customer number one thousand is, how you cross the chasm from early adopters, and why incumbents will concede the initial niche.
