Never estimate market size by taking one percent of an arbitrary multi-billion industry. Real sizing begins with countable discrete buying units multiplied by realistic annualized willingness to pay. State the exact number of qualified target buyers reachable in year one, what they spend on existing workarounds, and how your price captures that value. Top-down reports conceal zero-demand realities.
